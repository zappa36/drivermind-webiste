The problem

The best route data isn’t in your system.
It’s in your drivers’ heads.

Parcel logistics is highly automated — until the driver leaves the depot. From there, delivery depends on what the driver knows: where to park, which entrance works, how to reach the right door. That knowledge is captured nowhere, so the carrier pays for it three times over.

The friction drivers handle every day — invisible to the planning system

Ramp-up and churn

A new driver needs four to six weeks to reach full speed, and many don’t last that long. The knowledge leaves with them; the next hire starts from zero.

The same friction, relearned

Every driver rediscovers the entrances, parking spots and doors the last one already found — repeated across thousands of stops.

No insight into why

Dispatch sees that a stop ran late or failed, but never why. So tomorrow’s plan repeats today’s mistakes and the ETAs stay wrong.

When a driver hands in their keys, years of route knowledge walk out the door.

How it works

One simple loop. Three steps.

Step 1 · Capture

The driver says it

Drivers talk to Otto, a voice assistant — hands-free, while they work. No typing, no forms. Otto can also notice when something looks off, like an unusually long stop, and simply ask what happened.

Step 2 · Validate

AI structures it, a human approves

AI turns the voice note into a structured, location-tagged tip and matches it to the stop record. A dispatcher gives the final approval in one tap — human in the loop, minimal manual work.

Step 3 · Inherit

The next driver hears it

On the next run, the tip plays at the right stop, at the right moment. New drivers start with the knowledge of everyone who drove the route before them — and each shift sharpens what Otto asks next.

STRUCTURED FRICTION IN  →  BETTER ROUTES OUT

Approved tips do a second job: they feed back into route planning as structured, geo-referenced records — helping improve ETAs, stop order, and first-attempt success over time.

No speed scores

About places, not people

For carriers

What your operation gets

Faster ramp-up

New and temporary drivers inherit the route from day one, instead of spending four to six weeks learning it the hard way.

Known problems stop repeating

A broken bell or blocked access gets captured once — so the next driver doesn’t hit the same wall, and neither does the one after that.

A knowledge base you can trust

AI structures every tip and matches it to the stop record; a dispatcher gives the final approval. Trustworthy data, almost no manual work.

Planning that learns

Dispatch finally sees why a stop ran late or failed, not just that it did. Structured records feed route plans and ETA accuracy.

Ships inside your own app

Delivered as an SDK in the driver app your drivers already use — no second app to roll out. Sold per depot.

A data asset that compounds

Approved tips accumulate into a corpus a competitor can’t buy. It only grows by running real deliveries.
