The SDK

Otto plugs into the stack you already run.

Otto ships as an SDK inside your own driver app, on the devices your drivers already carry. No new app, no new login, no new hardware.

Inside your driver app

  • Plug-and-play: no changes to your front end or back end.
  • From in-house fleets to outsourced partners — a standalone app covers drivers who don't run your app.

Built for the van

  • Hands-free by design: audio over the vehicle speaker, the screen for a glance only.
  • Runs on the handhelds your fleet already buys, and speaks your drivers' languages.

Your data stays yours

  • The stop list stays in your app — it is not shared with ParcelVox.
  • The tips your drivers collect are yours alone — never shared with other parcel operators. Route knowledge stays your asset.
Beyond the driver report

What else Otto can do for you.

The driver report is where ParcelVox starts. These come next — each one by voice, each one inside your rules, and the final say stays with you.

Customer call

Otto calls your customer before the van arrives, checks someone will be home and agrees a time. Fewer stairs climbed for nothing, more deliveries that succeed on the first attempt.

Otto · to your customer“Understood. Your driver will come after six and hand it to you in person.”

Help from the office

Stuck at the door, your driver tells Otto once. Otto reaches the office with the stop and the problem already filled in, and comes back with the answer — even in the weeks when dispatch can't take another call.

Otto · to your driver“The new code is 4711. It's on file for the next driver.”

Driver check-in

Otto calls your driver for the office, one reason per call, and lets them drive. You decide when: trigger a check-in yourself, or set the rules and Otto makes the call for you.

Otto · to your driver“The customer at Kollwitzstraße 71 is away until tomorrow. Skip that stop today.”

On the driver's side

A colleague, not a supervisor.

Otto counts what your drivers share, never how fast they drive. There's no tracking of pace and no stops-per-hour league table. Drivers who feel watched stop talking. Drivers who feel helped tell Otto everything.
