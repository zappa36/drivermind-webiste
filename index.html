ParcelVox · Otto on the route

Meet Otto, the voice in the van.

ParcelVox puts a voice assistant inside your driver app. Drivers talk to it hands-free, like a colleague. Otto tells them what to expect at each stop, saves what they find for the next driver, and handles the calls to the office and the customer. Here is what Otto can do across one route, stop by stop.

Otto · stop by stop

Five moments on every route.

01On the way in

Pre-arrival notes

A few hundred metres out, Otto says the number, the name and what to expect at the door.

02Before moving on

The driver reports

The driver says what they found. Otto files it against the address for the next driver.

03At the door

Help from the office

The driver tells Otto once. Otto reaches the office and comes back with the answer.

04The plan changes

Driver check-in

Otto calls the driver for the office, one reason per call, and lets them drive.

05Before the van arrives

Customer call

Otto calls the customer, checks someone will be home and agrees a time.

Live demo

You play the driver.

  1. Press start — Otto appears in the corner of the page.
  2. Press the phone button in Otto's window and allow your microphone.

A live demo — no real depot behind it. Your microphone is used only during the call, and you can type instead of talking.

The SDK

Otto plugs into the stack you already run.

Otto ships as an SDK inside your own driver app, on the devices your drivers already carry. No new app, no new login, no new hardware.

Inside your driver app

  • Plug-and-play: no changes to your front end or back end.
  • From in-house fleets to outsourced partners — a standalone app covers drivers who don't run your app.

Built for the van

  • Hands-free by design: audio over the vehicle speaker, the screen for a glance only.
  • Runs on the handhelds your fleet already buys, and speaks your drivers' languages.

Your data stays yours

  • The stop list stays in your app — it is not shared with ParcelVox.
  • The tips your drivers collect are yours alone — never shared with other parcel operators. Route knowledge stays your asset.

On the driver's side

A colleague, not a supervisor.

Otto counts what your drivers share, never how fast they drive. There's no tracking of pace and no stops-per-hour league table. Drivers who feel watched stop talking. Drivers who feel helped tell Otto everything.
