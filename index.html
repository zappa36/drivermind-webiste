01 · Driver report

Ten minutes lost once. Never again.

The gate code has changed. There's a dog at the door. Scaffolding covers the entrance. Today, what a driver learns leaves with them.

With ParcelVox, your driver presses Otto, says what happened and moves on. Otto asks what the next driver needs to know, three questions at most. He files the tip against the address. The next time anyone drives to that stop, Otto reads the tip to them on the way in.

For your drivers

  • They report in a few sentences, by voice. No forms, no typing.
  • They hear what earlier drivers found before they reach the door.
  • Any route feels like their own route.

For your operation

  • The depot keeps what drivers know about each stop, not one driver.
  • Drivers spend less time searching at the door.
  • Cover drivers start with what the regular driver knows.

02 · Customer call

Find the empty home before your driver does.

Four floors, no lift, nobody in. Your driver climbs back down with the parcel and you pay for a second trip.

Otto calls your customer before the van arrives. He checks someone will be home and agrees a time. He follows your rules: fresh food goes into the customer's hands, never to a neighbour. If a stranger answers, Otto says nothing about the parcel. Then he tells your driver what was agreed.

For your drivers

  • Fewer stairs climbed for nothing.
  • No calls to customers from behind the wheel.
  • They arrive knowing someone is in.

For your operation

  • More deliveries succeed on the first attempt.
  • Fewer second trips.
  • Fresh-food and time-critical deliveries arrive when promised.
03 · Driver check-in

One short call. Hands on the wheel.

Dispatch rings to ask where the van is. The driver rings back to check a change. Neither call gets anyone a parcel closer to the door.

Otto calls your driver for the office, with one reason per call. He gets the answer, confirms it in one line and lets them drive.

For your drivers

  • Short calls that get to the point.
  • Clear instructions they can act on straight away.
  • No trips to stops that were cancelled an hour ago.

For your operation

  • You know who is running late without ringing round.
  • Changes reach the van as soon as they happen.

Stuck at the door? Otto gets the office.

The code on the label doesn't work. The address is wrong. The parcel won't fit the box. Today your driver waits on hold, then explains it all from the start.

With ParcelVox, your driver tells Otto once. Otto contacts the office with the stop, the customer and the problem already filled in. He comes back with the answer.

For your drivers

  • No hold music.
  • No explaining the problem twice.
  • They're back on the road sooner.

For your operation

  • Requests reach dispatch already sorted.
  • Every fix is filed against the address, so it only happens once.
